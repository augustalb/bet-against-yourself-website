How it works

  1. 1

    Set your alarm and your stake

    Pick a time, the days, and an amount between €5 and €100.

  2. 2

    Walk when it rings

    The alarm rings even on silent. Walk 100 steps within 5 minutes. Stop moving and it rings again.

  3. 3

    Keep your money, or lose it

    Made it? Nothing happens. Didn't? Your card is charged and you get a receipt.

Why it works

Losing money hurts about twice as much as winning the same amount feels good. Kahneman and Tversky called it loss aversion. So the app doesn't reward you for getting up. It takes money when you don't.

Questions

Is this gambling?

No. There's no chance involved and nothing to win. The outcome depends only on whether you get up. The best case is keeping your own money.

What if my phone dies?

If no successful result reaches us within 35 minutes of the alarm, the stake counts as lost. Charge your phone.

Can I cancel a stake?

Yes, for free until 2 hours before the alarm. After that it's locked.

Can I try it without paying?

Yes. The practice alarm rings in 60 seconds, with no money and no card.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone only. The app needs the iPhone's built-in step counter.
