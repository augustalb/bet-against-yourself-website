Why it works

Loss
aversion.

Losing money hurts about twice as much as winning the same amount feels good. Kahneman and Tversky showed it in 1979. Kahneman later won a Nobel Prize for it.

So we don't reward you for getting up. We take money when you don't.

Your brain will do anything to keep those 20 €. Even get up.

Questions

Excuses,
answered.

Is this gambling?

No. There's no chance involved and nothing to win. The outcome depends only on whether you get up. The best case is keeping your own money.

Where does the money go?

To us, the people who run the app. We say it openly so nobody feels tricked.

What if my phone dies?

If no successful result reaches us within 35 minutes of the alarm, the stake counts as lost. Charge your phone.

Can I cancel a stake?

Yes, for free until 2 hours before the alarm. After that it's locked – that's the point.

Can I try it without paying?

Yes. The practice alarm rings in 60 seconds, with no money and no card.

Which phones?

iPhone only. The app needs the iPhone's built-in step counter.
