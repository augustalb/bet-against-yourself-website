2. DATA WE COLLECT

Email address, name (if provided by Apple/Google sign-in) and authentication data (upon registration)

Wake-up times, time zone, bet amounts, step goals, and weekdays (to provide the service)

Challenge results: date, step count reported at the end of a challenge, and payment status

Payment data – card or Apple Pay (tokenized exclusively via Stripe — no raw data stored)

Technical data (device type, iOS version, app version for debugging)

Motion data (steps): Counted on your device via Apple Core Motion. Only the step count at the end of a challenge is transmitted to our servers to determine whether the stake is charged. No continuous motion or health data is stored.

3. PURPOSE OF DATA PROCESSING

We process your data solely to:

– Provide app features (alarm, step counting, payment processing)

– Communicate in response to support requests

– Improve the app (usage statistics linked to your account ID)

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contractual performance) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests).

4. THIRD-PARTY PROVIDERS

Supabase (Ireland / USA): Authentication and database storage. Data processing based on standard contractual clauses.

Stripe, Inc. (USA): Payment processing (card and Apple Pay). Stripe is PCI-DSS certified. Privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy

PostHog, Inc. (USA): Usage statistics (app events such as "challenge started"), linked to your account ID. Data processing based on standard contractual clauses.

Apple Core Motion: Step counting on your device.

5. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

Account data is deleted as soon as you delete your account in the app or send us a deletion request. Payment histories are retained in accordance with statutory retention obligations (10 years).

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection. Contact us at august.albrecht@gmx.de. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority.

All data is transmitted in encrypted form (TLS). Passwords are not stored in plain text. Payment data is held exclusively by Stripe.
